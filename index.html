SACP Councillor Poster Portal

Verify your details against the authorised IEC candidate export, confirm them, then create your poster.

1. Verify identity against IEC export

First, you can look up your certified candidate name and ward in The People’s Register. Then enter your ID number for a separate match against the authorised candidate export imported by the administrator. This portal does not log in to IEC and does not claim live IEC verification.

Open The People’s Register — City of Tshwane candidates ↗

The public register is a reference for candidate names, parties and wards. It is not an identity-check service and does not replace the authorised ID-record match below.

2. Confirm your candidate details

Compare the displayed details with your official information. If anything is wrong, do not confirm; contact the administrator.
